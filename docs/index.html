Aegython 2026 · Track 2: Find the Anomaly

Find the anomaly in an option chain, and see exactly why it was flagged

A single options expiry is a wall of numbers: 31 strikes, each with a call and a put, each with its own open interest, implied volatility and volume. That is 186 values for one value out of line to hide among, and in practice nobody has labels saying which ones are wrong. So a useful detector has to work without labels, declare its rules in advance and explain every flag.

ATHENA does that with three plain statistical checks. To measure it honestly, the generator plants three labelled anomalies in each synthetic chain. The detector never sees those labels; they are used only to mark its answers.

How the detection works

  1. 1. Predict the normal value

    OI: the median of the 3 strikes either side (on log OI). IV: a quadratic smile fitted across all strikes. Volume: the chain's typical log(volume ÷ OI). The gap between the actual and predicted value is the residual.

  2. 2. Measure how unusual the gap is

    Each residual becomes a robust z-score: 0.6745 × (residual − median) ÷ MAD. The median and MAD barely move when one value is extreme, so an outlier can't hide by inflating the spread, as it can with a mean and standard deviation.

  3. 3. Flag it against a declared threshold

    OI spike: z > 5.0. IV kink: |z| > 3.5. Volume burst: z > 3.5. Chosen on tuning seeds 0–49 only, then scored on seeds that played no part in choosing them. Every flag shows its score and reason.

How to read this page
Robust z-score
How many typical spreads a value sits from normal. Higher means stranger. It is a ranking, not a probability.
✓ Hit, ✕ False flag, ○ Missed
Hit: flagged and planted. False flag: flagged but not planted. Missed: planted but under the threshold.
Dots and rings
Blue dots are calls and orange dots are puts. A yellow ring marks a flag, a dashed ring marks a miss, and the dashed line is the threshold.
Seeds
A seed fixes every random draw, so one seed always gives one chain. 0–49 tuned the thresholds; 1000–1199 are held out. Results vary by seed: try the failure examples.
Open interest (OI), volume
OI is the number of contracts still open. Volume is the number traded in the session.
Implied volatility (IV), smile
IV is the volatility implied by an option's price. Plotted across strikes it forms a smooth curve, the smile, so a sudden break stands out.
ATM, call (CE), put (PE)
ATM is the strike nearest the index level. CE and PE are the exchange's labels for calls and puts.
What this is not
Everything is synthetic, so a flag here says nothing about any real market, and nothing here is a suggestion to trade.

Limitations

  • The data is synthetic and cleaner than a real market, so these scores do not predict performance on real chains.
  • Test seeds come from the same generator as the tuning seeds. They test for overfitting to particular random draws, not for a change in the data's structure.
  • IV kink precision is the weakest result: its false flags mostly occur away from round-number strikes.
  • The OI detector misses small spikes, as the stress table shows, because its threshold is set high: round-number strikes legitimately carry more OI than their neighbours.
